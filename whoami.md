I'm Hans, a Flemish engineer living and working in Wallonia. I work at Group Fruytier
in Marche-en-Famenne, on projects for glulam and CLT production and sawmill machinery.

I'm not a professional programmer, and I don't plan to become one. I use AI, mostly
Claude, to build practical tools for my own work: small Python apps, reports, and
training manuals. I want to get the most out of AI at my own level, and to show that
you don't need to be a developer to do useful things with it.

I work in Dutch, English and French every day.

If you work in the timber industry, or you use AI in an industrial setting without a
programming background, I'm happy to share experience. You can reach me on LinkedIn
or by email.
